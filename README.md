## 📏 Registration Requirements

- **Minimum Name Length:** All extensions require a subdomain name of at least **4 characters (4, 5, 6, or more)**.
- **IDN Support:** Internationalized Domain Names (IDN) are supported, allowing names in non-English scripts and languages.
- **Free Zones:** Registration does not require an IFP Key.
- **Key Zones:** Exactly **1 IFP Key** is required for each domain registration.

---

## 💎 Short & Premium Domains

By default, all registrations require a minimum of 4 characters.

If you are working on a high-quality project or would like a **1, 2, or 3-character domain**, please contact the administrators through our **Discord server**.

Short-domain requests are manually reviewed to prevent domain squatting and ensure that valuable names are assigned to genuine, active projects.

## 🛠️ How to Get Your Domain

1. **Check Availability:** Use our [WHOIS Lookup](https://panel.int.yt/public-whois.php) to check whether your desired name is available.
2. **Create an Account:** Sign up at the [Intent Dashboard](https://panel.int.yt/signup.php).
3. **Register:** Choose your preferred extension and claim your domain through the user dashboard.
4. **Connect DNS:** Point your domain's NS (nameserver) records to your hosting provider.

**Supported DNS Providers:** FreeDNS, Namecheap.

**Coming Soon:** Intent-DNS support.
