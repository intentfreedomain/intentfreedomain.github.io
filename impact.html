Making the Web More Accessible

A domain name is often one of the first steps toward building something on the internet. Intent provides free subdomains so that developers, students, creators, and project communities can establish an online identity without needing to purchase a traditional domain first.

Our goal is to reduce the initial barriers to experimentation and help more people participate in building on the web.

🎓

Students can create websites and technical projects without needing to purchase a domain before they are ready.

Communities and independent projects can use subdomains to create dedicated web destinations for their work and resources.

Free Access to DNS

Intent FreeDomain is designed around providing useful DNS and subdomain functionality without requiring users to purchase a traditional domain simply to get started.

This makes the service particularly useful for early-stage projects, learning, experimentation, personal websites, and open-source development.

Supporting an Open Web

We believe the ability to create and publish on the internet should be accessible to more people.

By providing free subdomains and a simple DNS management experience, Intent aims to support a more accessible environment for people who want to learn, build, experiment, and share their work online.

Community-Driven Development

Intent is supported by a community of users, developers, and contributors. Feedback and contributions help us identify problems, improve the service, and determine which features are most useful.

If you want to contribute to the project, you can participate through our GitHub organization or join the Intent Discord community.

Our Current Reach

Intent currently serves more than 3,000 users and has facilitated more than 4,000 domains.

These figures represent the current reach of the service and may change as more people join and create domains through Intent.

Looking Ahead

As Intent grows, we aim to continue improving the reliability, accessibility, and usefulness of our services while keeping the experience simple for developers, students, creators, and communities.

Our focus remains on building useful infrastructure that helps more people create and participate on the web.
