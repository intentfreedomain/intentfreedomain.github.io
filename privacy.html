Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Intent™ ("Intent", "we", "our" or "us") respects the privacy of individuals who use our websites, dashboard, domain registration services and related infrastructure.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected, how it is used, how it is protected, when it may be shared and what choices may be available to users.

Important: Intent provides free subdomain registration and related services. Although domain registration information is required to operate and protect the service, we do not intentionally publish users' private registration details through our public WHOIS interface.

1. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to information processed in connection with:

  • The official Intent website at int.yt.
  • The current Intent Dashboard at panel.int.yt.
  • Account creation and authentication.
  • Subdomain registration and management.
  • DNS and nameserver-related services.
  • IFP Key purchases and related transactions.
  • Customer support and abuse reporting.
  • Security investigations and infrastructure protection.

This policy does not automatically govern independent websites, hosting providers, external DNS providers or other third-party services that users choose to connect to their domains.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features used and information required during registration, Intent may collect the following categories of information.

2.1 Account Information

  • Username or account identifier.
  • Legal name, where requested during registration.
  • Email address.
  • Mobile number, where requested.
  • Address or other registration details, where requested.
  • Account creation date and account status.
  • Authentication and account-security records.

2.2 Information You Provide

We may receive information you voluntarily submit through support requests, contact forms, abuse reports, security reports, feedback or other communication channels.

This information may include your name, email address, message contents, attachments and information relevant to the request.

2.3 Information From Authentication Providers

If external authentication providers are offered and you choose to use them, we may receive information made available through the authentication process, such as an account identifier, username or email address, subject to the provider's permissions and settings.

3. Technical and Security Information

When you access our services, certain technical information may be collected automatically for operational and security purposes.

  • IP addresses.
  • Browser type and version.
  • Operating system and device information.
  • Request timestamps.
  • Login and authentication events.
  • Failed login attempts.
  • Session and security-related identifiers.
  • Pages and service endpoints accessed.
  • Errors and diagnostic information.

This information helps us investigate suspicious activity, prevent automated abuse, identify unauthorized access and maintain service reliability.

4. Domain and DNS Information

When you register or manage a subdomain through Intent, we process information necessary to provide that service.

This may include:

  • Your registered subdomain and selected extension.
  • Registration and modification timestamps.
  • Domain status and account association.
  • Nameserver records.
  • DNS records and their values.
  • Domain configuration and management history.
  • Relevant DNS activity and security events.

DNS information may be publicly resolvable depending on the record type and configuration. Users should not place passwords, authentication tokens or other confidential information in publicly accessible DNS records.

5. IFP Keys and Payment Information

Intent may offer IFP Keys for eligible domain registrations and other features.

When you purchase or use IFP Keys, we may process:

  • Purchase and transaction references.
  • Number of keys purchased or used.
  • Purchase timestamps and transaction status.
  • Relevant account and order identifiers.
  • Payment verification and fraud-prevention information.

Payments may be handled through external payment providers. Those providers may independently collect and process payment information under their own privacy policies.

We do not claim to store complete payment card details unless this is explicitly part of an enabled payment integration and its disclosed processing arrangement.

6. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and maintain user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and protect account access.
  • Register and manage subdomains.
  • Configure and maintain DNS services.
  • Process IFP Key transactions and registrations.
  • Provide technical support.
  • Prevent spam, phishing, malware and fraud.
  • Detect unauthorized access and suspicious activity.
  • Investigate violations of our policies.
  • Respond to legitimate legal requests.
  • Maintain infrastructure reliability and performance.
  • Communicate important service-related notices.
  • Protect users, infrastructure and the community.

7. Cookies and Sessions

Our websites and dashboard may use cookies or similar technologies to support essential website functionality.

These technologies may be used for:

  • Maintaining authenticated sessions.
  • Protecting against cross-site request forgery.
  • Remembering necessary security preferences.
  • Supporting login and account functionality.

Browser settings may allow you to restrict or delete cookies. Disabling essential cookies may prevent certain dashboard functions from working correctly.

If optional analytics or other tracking technologies are introduced, their use should be disclosed appropriately.

8. Public WHOIS and Privacy

Intent provides a public WHOIS lookup service to allow visitors to check information about registered subdomains.

Our public WHOIS presentation is designed to avoid unnecessarily exposing individual users' private registration information.

Depending on the domain and available records, public WHOIS results may display information such as:

  • The registered domain name.
  • Registration date.
  • Domain status.
  • Nameserver information, where available.
  • Managed-by information identifying Intent.
  • A service contact address such as admin@int.yt.

Privacy distinction: Public WHOIS information is different from internal account and registration records. Intent may retain the original registration information internally for legitimate operational, security and compliance purposes even when it is not displayed publicly.

We may disclose additional information where required by applicable law or where a valid legal or security obligation requires it.

9. Information Sharing

Intent does not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers.

We may share limited information in the following circumstances:

9.1 Infrastructure Providers

Information may be processed by hosting providers, infrastructure providers, DNS providers and other technical service providers when necessary to operate the service.

9.2 Payment Providers

Relevant transaction information may be shared with payment providers to process purchases, verify transactions and prevent payment fraud.

9.3 Abuse and Security Investigations

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary to investigate phishing, malware, fraud, copyright infringement, security incidents or other serious abuse.

9.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information in response to valid legal processes, court orders, lawful government requests or other legally binding obligations.

9.5 Protection of Rights and Infrastructure

Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, property and infrastructure of Intent, its users or other affected parties.

Any sharing should be limited to information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

10. Third-Party Services

Intent may rely on or link to external services, including hosting companies, DNS providers, payment processors, authentication providers, communication platforms and other infrastructure services.

These services may have their own privacy policies, security practices and data-processing arrangements.

Intent does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party websites or providers. Users should review their policies before using them.

11. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, destruction and misuse.

Security measures may include:

  • Access controls and account permissions.
  • Authentication and session protections.
  • Infrastructure and application security measures.
  • Security monitoring and abuse detection.
  • Restricted access to sensitive administrative data.
  • Investigation of suspected security incidents.

No internet service, storage system or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We therefore cannot promise absolute security.

12. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

Retention periods may depend on:

  • Whether your account remains active.
  • Whether a subdomain remains registered.
  • Operational and infrastructure requirements.
  • Security investigations and abuse reports.
  • Financial recordkeeping obligations.
  • Applicable legal requirements.
  • The need to resolve disputes or enforce policies.

Different information categories may have different retention periods. Certain records may be retained after account closure where a legitimate purpose or legal obligation requires it.

13. Account Closure and Deletion

Users may contact Intent to request account closure or deletion of personal information associated with their account.

We may need to verify the identity and authority of the requester before processing such a request.

Closing an account does not necessarily result in the immediate deletion of every associated record.

Certain information may be retained where necessary for security, abuse prevention, transaction records, legal compliance, dispute resolution or legitimate operational purposes.

Account closure may also result in the suspension, removal or release of associated subdomains in accordance with our Terms of Service.

14. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances of processing, you may have rights concerning your personal information.

These may include:

  • Requesting access to personal information.
  • Requesting correction of inaccurate information.
  • Requesting deletion where legally available.
  • Withdrawing consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Raising objections to certain processing activities.
  • Submitting complaints to an appropriate authority.

We may request additional information to verify a privacy request and may be unable to fulfil requests that conflict with applicable legal obligations.

To submit a privacy request, contact us using the details provided below.

15. International Processing

Intent and its infrastructure providers may operate in or use systems located in different countries.

Consequently, information may be processed or stored outside the country in which a user resides.

Where applicable, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that international processing complies with relevant legal requirements.

16. Children's Privacy

Our services are not designed to encourage children to submit unnecessary personal information.

Users must not provide personal information belonging to another person without appropriate authority. Where applicable law requires parental or guardian involvement or consent, those requirements must be followed.

If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, please contact us.

17. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, infrastructure, operational practices or applicable legal requirements.

The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised last-updated date.

Where required, we will provide additional notice of material changes.
