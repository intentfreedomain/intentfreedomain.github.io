IFP Keys are not domain names, ownership rights, cryptocurrency, bank deposits or a guarantee of permanent service availability.

8. Domain Names and IDN Support

Standard registrations require a minimum subdomain name length of four characters.

Names containing four, five, six or more characters may be registered, subject to availability and technical validation.

Internationalized Domain Names (IDNs) are supported where the registration system and relevant DNS infrastructure permit them.

IDN names may be represented internally using compatible DNS encoding formats, including Punycode where applicable.

Requests for one-, two- or three-character names may be considered through manual administrative review. Approval is discretionary and is not guaranteed.

Manual approval does not override trademark, impersonation, abuse or security restrictions.
